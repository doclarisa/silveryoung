## HOW IT WORKS

## Modeling, made simple

**1. Share your style**
Create your profile in about 5 minutes. Add three photos taken on your phone, and tell us your size and city.

**2. A brand picks your look**
Fashion brands browse our members and send requests like "Women 60+ to model our new fall sweaters." When you're a match, you'll hear from us.

**3. Get the clothes and get paid**
The brand ships you their clothes. You take the photos at home or anywhere you love. You keep the clothes and get paid for your photos.

## WHY JOIN

## Why you'll love it

**No experience? Perfect.** You don't need a portfolio, an agent or a "model look." Real style is exactly what brands are looking for.

**New clothes, real pay.** Every brand shoot comes with clothes to keep and payment for your photos.

**Your photos, your choice.** For every photo, you decide: show it to everyone, or only to brands.

## PRIVACY PROMISE

## Safe, private and always respectful

- We only show your first name and city, never your address, email or phone.
- You choose who sees each photo.
- Brands contact you only through our site, never directly.
- You can change or delete your profile anytime.

## FAQ

## Questions? We have answers.

**Does it cost anything?**
No. Joining is completely free.

**Do I need professional photos?**
Not at all. Phone photos taken in daylight are perfect. We'll show you simple tips.

**What if I've never modeled before?**
Most of our members haven't! If you enjoy clothes and a little fun in front of the camera, you're ready.

**Who are the brands?**
We're partnering with clothing brands that dress women over 50. As Founding Members join, the first brand requests are on their way.

**Is there an age limit?**
Our members are 50 and up, with no upper limit. Style only gets better.
